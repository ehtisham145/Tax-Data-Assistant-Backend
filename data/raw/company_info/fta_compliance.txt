Compliance Pillars
How We Meet FTA Requirements
Every layer of the E-Numerak platform is engineered to satisfy UAE e-invoicing regulations.
BIS 3.0 / UBL 2.1 Format
Every invoice is generated as a structured UBL 2.1 XML document that conforms to the UAE PINT (Peppol International) billing specification.
Digital Signing
Invoices are digitally signed with accredited PKI certificates, ensuring authenticity, integrity, and non-repudiation as required by the FTA.
5-Corner Model
Invoices are transmitted through an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) and routed to the buyer and the FTA following the UAE 5-corner e-invoicing model.
Record Retention
Tax records are retained for the period mandated by UAE law, with full audit trails on every invoice action.
PKI & Encryption
End-to-end encryption and PKI-based digital signatures ensure invoice integrity and non-repudiation throughout the transmission lifecycle.
Full Audit Trail
Every invoice action — creation, validation, signing, submission — is logged with timestamps and user identity for complete traceability.
Regulatory Timeline
UAE E-Invoicing Roadmap
The UAE e-invoicing mandate is being rolled out in phases to ensure a smooth transition for all businesses operating in the country.
E-Numerak is designed to support taxpayers at every stage of the rollout, from early adoption through full compliance.
2024
Federal Decree-Law No. 16 mandates e-invoicing in the UAE
2025
Phase 1 — B2B & B2G e-invoicing goes live for large taxpayers
2026
Phase 2 — Middle segment and SME adoption period
2027
Full compliance — all taxable persons required to use e-invoicing
Regulatory Basis
Built on UAE Law
UAE e-invoicing is mandated under Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2024. Phase 1 covers B2B and B2G transactions. E-Numerak generates compliant structured invoices, signs them, and transmits them to the FTA through accredited service providers — so your business stays compliant without the complexity.
